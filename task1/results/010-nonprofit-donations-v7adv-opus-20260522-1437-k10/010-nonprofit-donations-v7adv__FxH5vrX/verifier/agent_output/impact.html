// 08METHODOLOGY

○ FIELD ATTESTATION

Each project is monitored on a defined cadence: quarterly drone survey, annual species census, biennial soil-column draw. The data, raw, is posted to the open ledger within fourteen days of each survey window. Attestation is signed by an independent ecologist who is not on the foundry's payroll.

○ FINANCIAL ATTESTATION

Marchant & Cole, our external auditor, signs the full ledger annually against ISA 720 with extended scope. Their letter is filed as Appendix C of this ledger. We publish the auditor's working notes on request, with personally identifying information redacted per UK GDPR.

○ FORECAST BRACKETS

Every project carries a forecast bracket — the upper and lower bound on its expected outcome at year 5, set at the time of underwriting. Where the bracket is missed, we publish the variance, the cause, and the remedy. We do not bury misses.

○ ROUNDING & CARRY

All figures are stated to two significant places against a forecast bracket. Cash carries are recorded against the project that generated them, not against the foundry pool. We charge no carry, no commission, no exit fee.

// 10A NOTE ON COSTS

€8,346 / ha

FY24 blended cost per hectare brought under stewardship. The number averages across biomes; coastal saltmarsh sits at €12,420/ha, peat at €5,180/ha, old-growth at €4,910/ha.

All-in: title, covenant filing, planting and propagation, three years of monitoring, audit, and overhead.

€17.40 / ha · year

FY24 long-run cost of holding land under restoration covenant per hectare, per year. Holds steady from FY23.

All-in: monitoring, occasional reseeding, lease payments where applicable, and the foundry's annual share of overhead.
