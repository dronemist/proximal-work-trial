// 02HOW THE FOUNDRY WORKS
FULL CHARTER PDF →

We finance the slow work of pulling landscapes back to function. Our patrons — individual underwriters, family trusts, and aligned institutions — pool capital into a single revolving fund. From that fund we issue restoration capital to land-stewards holding a defensible, multi-decadal plan against a defined hectare-count.

Restoration capital is not a grant. Each disbursement is bound by a covenant: monitored outcomes, public ledger entries, and a return-of-stewardship clause if the work lapses. We do not own the land. We finance the slow re-functioning of it.

Where outcomes outperform the bracket, the steward retains the upside. Where the project ends, the title reverts to the local trust, never to the foundry. We charge no carry, no commission. Our overheads run at 4.6% of throughput, audited annually.

○ CAPITAL FORMATION

Quarterly subscription cycles, with rolling underwriting between. The minimum patron entry is £500; the median individual subscription is £4,200.

○ DISBURSEMENT

Releases against milestones. A milestone is a verifiable physical state of the land. Forecast budgets are public; variance is published quarterly.

○ MEASUREMENT

Every site is monitored on a fixed cadence — quarterly drone survey, annual species census, biennial soil column. Our methods are open.

○ ATTESTATION

Each year, an external auditor signs the outcome ledger. The ledger is public. Errors are amended in place with a printed correction footnote.

○ LAND FIRST, NARRATIVE SECOND

42.4% of every pound is directed at securing the title to a defensible patch of land. We start with covenant, easement, or fee-simple. Without title, the work doesn't survive a property generation.

○ PERMANENCE BY COVENANT

We are wary of any restoration that lives only as long as a contract. The covenants we use bind in title and run with the land — not with the trustee. A successor cannot undo the work.

○ UNDERWROTE AGAINST OUTCOMES

Disbursement is tied to a defined milestone — usually a state of the land, attested by an independent ecologist. Capital releases on attestation; not on plan-signing.

○ NO GREENWASHING CREDITS

We do not sell carbon credits, biodiversity units, or compliance offsets against our work. Where outcomes are measured, they belong to the land, to the steward, and to the public ledger.

○ LOCAL HANDS, LOCAL PAYROLL

Staffing on the ground is hired through the local steward, not seconded from the foundry. We pay local payroll above the regional median, never below.

○ PUBLIC LEDGER & IT DOESN'T BURGEON

The full ledger is open: project-level spend, hectare-level outcomes, audit footnotes. The ledger is also bounded — when work concludes, the file is closed and indexed.
