Quarterly report 22 May 2026 · Cardigan Bay

Three years on, the Cardigan Bay saltmarsh is holding sediment faster than projected.

The seven hectares enclosed in spring 2023 are accreting at 11.4 mm of vertical gain per tidal cycle — well above the conservative forecast we drafted with University of Aberystwyth's coastal lab and reported in the 2024 prospectus.

What surprised the team was the early return of Salicornia europaea across the inner basin. Pages 4–11 of the field log set the data alongside drone transects and a frankly muddy account from the wardens.
