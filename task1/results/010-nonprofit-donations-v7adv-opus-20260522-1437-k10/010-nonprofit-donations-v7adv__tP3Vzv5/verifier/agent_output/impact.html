05METHODOLOGYRead time · 7 min
A1

Field measurement

Hectares are measured in two ways: by initial tenure, and by audited recovery against a forecast band agreed with a partner university before planting.

A2

Capital accounting

All deployed capital is recorded on a public ledger and double-entered. The foundry holds no off-ledger accounts; every account is signed-audit each February.

A3

Survival rates

Survival is reported at year-three, then at year-seven, then at year-fifteen. The intermediate years are noted but treated as transient.

A4

Community attribution

Where local people, including subsistence users, are working alongside the foundry, hours and craft are recorded — but never monetised in this report.
