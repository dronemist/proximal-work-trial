FIG.01 — TIDAL UPTAKE, CARDIGAN BAY · MAR ‑ MAY 24
RESTORATION CARBON CARDIGAN BAY · EDITED BY E.MOREL

Three years on, the Cardigan Bay saltmarsh is holding sediment faster than projected.

When we first underwrote the Cardigan rewetting in 2021, the modelled accretion curve was generous — 4.1 mm/yr against a 3.2 mm baseline. The basin has now logged 11.2 mm in the most recent monitoring window, an unhurried but unmistakable departure. We spent eight days walking transects with the Powys stewards and reading the sediment traps. What we found was not a miracle, only a slow refusal to stop working.

The Foundry's continued commitment to this place is a matter of patience capital — a fund that does not flinch when the line on the chart goes briefly the wrong way. Read on for the full ledger entry, hectare-by-hectare uptake, and the partner brief from Carreg-y-Llan.
