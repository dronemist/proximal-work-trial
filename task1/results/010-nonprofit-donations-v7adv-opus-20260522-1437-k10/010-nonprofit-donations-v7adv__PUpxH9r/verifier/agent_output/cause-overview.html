§01 About the cause
EDIT BRIEF PRINT

We finance the slow, unglamorous work of putting land back into a working ecological condition. The model is patient: capital is allocated against a multi-decadal covenant, disbursed annually against monitored outcomes, and the Foundry will not exit. We measure permanence in flora that returns under its own arrangement, in sediment retained, in water tables that quietly recover their habit.

Cause #04 operates across four biomes — coastal saltmarsh, riparian wetland, low-elevation broadleaf, and montane peat — selected for their disproportionate ecological return per hectare of capital deployed. The Foundry's discipline is that the same hectare must remain in stewardship across 50 years, and the partner trust must be content with a quiet century, not a noisy decade.

§03 Key actions funded
FY24 · ACROSS 27 PROJECTS
◇

Land first, narrative second

We close on the title or easement before we publish anything. Eight new parcels acquired this fiscal year, total 1,180 hectares.

≋

Permanence by covenant

Every parcel is bound by a recorded restoration covenant. Median term: 99 years. Two parcels in perpetuity.

↺

Hydrology against subsidence

Rewetting peat, reflooding marsh, removing tile drains. 318 ha of hydrologically restored ground added this quarter.

⌖

No greenwashing instruments

The Foundry does not issue offsets, credits, or speculative instruments. Donations are flat, and the ledger is plain.

⌥

Local hands, local payroll

Wardens, stewards, monitors paid at full local salary against a 5-year minimum contract, plus a permanence stipend.

✶

Public ledger & field reports

Every disbursement and every monitoring outcome is published. Two-thirds of our donors have walked a parcel.
