§08 Methodology
FULL TEXT →
EU-RES.04

Field measured

Hectares restored are counted only after a second-quarter monitoring window with documented vegetative return, not at the moment of planting or rewetting.

UNIT · HECTARE · CONSERVATIVE
EU-FIN.02

Conservative accounting

Euro amounts are counted at the moment of disbursement, not at the moment of pledge. The Foundry does not capitalise pledged future giving.
