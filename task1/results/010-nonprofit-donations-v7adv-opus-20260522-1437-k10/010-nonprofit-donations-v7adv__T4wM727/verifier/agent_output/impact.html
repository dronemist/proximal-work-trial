§ 09

Methodology

Filed alongside the ledger
01

Field measurement

Every project carries a contracted, in-the-field measurement plan against which the milestones can be drawn. We do not pay against intentions; we pay against measurements.

02

Financial reconciliation

Treasury closes the books on a monthly cadence with three-way reconciliation: bank, ledger, programme report. Audited annually by an external firm rotated every five years.

03

Community attestation

Where land is held in trust by an indigenous council or local cooperative, every line item is published in the council's own language alongside English, no later than 30 days after deployment.

04

Donor accounting

No restricted gift is comingled with general funds. The donor receipt names the project, the milestone, and the audit window in which the gift will be deployed.
