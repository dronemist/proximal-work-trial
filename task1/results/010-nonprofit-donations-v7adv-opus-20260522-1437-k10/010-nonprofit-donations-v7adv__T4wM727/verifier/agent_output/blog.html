Salt marsh Cardigan Bay Field report

Three years on, the Cardigan Bay saltmarsh is holding sediment faster than projected.

Our hydrology contractor wrapped the third annual sediment survey last week. The averaged accretion rate across the four monitored plots is now 8.2 mm/year — well above the 5.5 mm/year baseline we used when modelling site viability in 2022.

It's a modest but meaningful proof point: the dyke breach we funded with the Llanrhystud parish is, by every measurable, doing what it was supposed to do.
