02 — Cause profile Restoration Capital Methodology v3.4

We finance the slow part of restoration: the years between a parcel being purchased and a parcel being measurably ecologically richer. Most of our grants are 5–9 years long; the longest is 22. We do not subsidise short campaigns or one-off plantings — there are foundations far better suited to that work.

The cause holds a working balance of approximately £26M across endowment, recoverable bridge loans, and discretionary deployment. Returns to the endowment are re-cycled into project work the following year, less a 2.6% administrative draw.

Stewardship is delegated to the 23-person field team and partner land trusts. The foundry retains spending authority but not field decisions; we trust the people on the ground to know what their site needs.
