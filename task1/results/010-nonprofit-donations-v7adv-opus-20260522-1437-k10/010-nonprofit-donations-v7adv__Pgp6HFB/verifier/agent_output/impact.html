Methodology

Full v3.4 methodology →
Field measurement

Hectare counts are measured by polygon survey, not extrapolation. Every parcel referenced in this ledger has been walked, photographed, and instrumented to v3.4 standard.

Financial accounting

Bruyn & Calley audit our books quarterly under the EU non-profit framework. The bank-to-bank trail for every line item in this ledger is reproducible from the public CSV.

Steward attribution

Every hour logged is attributed to a named steward and a project site. We don't aggregate volunteer hours, donated equipment, or "in-kind" estimates into restoration totals.

Public reproducibility

All raw measurement files for FY24 are mirrored on the open-data archive. If a figure in this report doesn't reconcile to a CSV row, please write us.
