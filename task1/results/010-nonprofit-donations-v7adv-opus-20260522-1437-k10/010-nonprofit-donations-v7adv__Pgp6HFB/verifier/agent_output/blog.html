Sediment Cardigan Bay
PLOT 7 · 2022 → 2026, +0.42m
Saltmarsh Quarterly 5 min read

Three years on, the Cardigan Bay saltmarsh is holding sediment faster than projected.

When we re-flooded the Cae Newydd flats in spring 2023, models forecast accretion of 6–9mm/yr — comfortable for a healthy estuary, but slow. Three monitoring seasons later the marsh is taking on 14mm/yr in the outer cells and 18mm at the inflow. The aerial transect shows the littoral edge has moved seaward by 27 meters since the last quarterly flyover.

By H. Aldridge & team · March 18, 2026 · Dispatch 142 / 2026
