QUARTERLY LETTER FIELD REPORT CARDIGAN BAY

Three years on, the Cardigan Bay saltmarsh is holding sediment faster than projected.

The pilot reach at Mwnt has cleared its third growing season under managed inundation. Accretion ledgers — measured against the 2021 baseline — show 4.6 cm of net deposit, ahead of the 3.1 cm modelled. We log what shifted, what didn't, and what we expect to do differently in Q4.
