Holding the line on the Mwnt tidal mar­gin · 4.6 cm

Six fortuna estuary returns 1,372 ha to tidal exchange

The southern berm was breached on schedule in March. Twelve weeks later, daily inundation has stabilised at expected amplitude and salinity gradient.

08METHODOLOGY how we counted what we counted
M-01

Field measurement

Every site is walked at least once per calendar year by a member of the field team. Soil cores are pulled at 12-point grids on saltmarsh sites, 24-point on peat. We use a single laboratory (Pencader Soils) to remove inter-lab drift.

Hydrology is monitored continuously where instrumentation is present; otherwise we use spot readings on the audit walk.

FULL METHOD NOTE ↗
M-02

Carbon accounting

We use IPCC AR6 default factors for above-ground biomass and bespoke factors for saltmarsh accretion calibrated from our own deposition cores. We do not net out carbon from displaced agricultural activity.

Our soil carbon estimates carry a stated ±18% uncertainty band, reflecting field-sampling variance more than measurement error.
