02WHAT WE DO

The Foundry was founded in 2012 by a coalition of landholders, hydrologists, and a small finance bench, with the working premise that ecological restoration runs on a different clock than philanthropic capital usually allows. Most of what works in degraded land — letting beavers come back, plugging a peat ridge, allowing a saltmarsh to inundate — takes a decade or more before the figure on the page changes.

So we built an instrument that holds capital across that horizon. Donors give to a trust that pre-commits funds to named projects on five- to fifteen-year drawdown schedules. Each pound is tied to a hectare, a method, and a measurable. Each quarter we publish what changed on the ground and what we got wrong.

The trust is small on purpose. We currently work across 21 watersheds in Wales, the Border Mires, and a contiguous block of forest in the Carpathian range. Capacity is roughly twelve new projects per year. We do not run general-purpose grants.

04TAXONOMY OF OUTCOMES how we choose to measure
◆◇ LAND

Land first, narrative second

We measure standing biomass, soil carbon, water-table position, and species return. We let the photographs come later.

◆◇ EVIDENCE

Evidence against orthodoxy

If the ledger contradicts the prevailing method, we publish the ledger. We have closed three projects this way.

◆◇ TIME

No greenwashing instruments

No carbon credits, no tokenised hectares, no co-mingled vehicles. Capital is direct, named, and slow.

◆◇ AUDIT

Public ledger, 5-year auditable

The hectare ledger is published in full each quarter. Donors can trace a pound to a plot and a measurable.
