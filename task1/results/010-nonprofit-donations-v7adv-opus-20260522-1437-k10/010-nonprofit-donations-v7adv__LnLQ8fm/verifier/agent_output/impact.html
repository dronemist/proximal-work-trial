07Methodology

Full paper →

FIELD MEASUREMENTS

Every site has a six-year axis.

Before any disbursement clears, the project signs a measurement plan: what we will measure, on what cadence, to what tolerance, and what specific findings would cause us to withdraw funding. The plan is published with the project, not buried in an annex, and is held to in audit.

CAPITAL ACCOUNTING

Public ledger, daily close.

Every euro out of the foundry is published to the public ledger by close of next business day. The ledger is human-readable, machine-readable, and unchangeable after entry. Reconciliations against bank ledgers are run twice monthly.

COMMUNITY ATTRIBUTION

Outcomes belong to the place.

We don't claim outcomes that other groups produced or supported. Co-funded projects are co-attributed. Where local stewards run the work, they hold the narrative; we hold only the spreadsheet.

WITHDRAWAL PROTOCOL

The "no" file is public.

Projects that fail their six-year axis are wound down. The reasoning, the data, and the cost of the wind-down are published. Withdrawal is uncommon but ordinary; the file is the most-read section of the year.
