Three years on, the Cardigan Bay saltmarsh is holding sediment faster than projected.

When we re-opened the back-channels at Ynys-las in spring 2023, the working assumption was that accretion would lag for at least five seasons. Instead, the upper marsh is gaining 12 mm of fresh silt a year — roughly double the conservative scenario in the original modelling memo.

This entry walks through what the monitoring stations have actually recorded, where the volunteer transects diverge from the LIDAR, and the methodological corrections we now think the broader restoration community should adopt.
