07Methodology
Read in full ↗

Field measurement

Every project on the ledger is measured monthly, with a structured field-data return signed by both the steward and a community-verified observer.

Field-data returns and raw observation logs are published on the live ledger at ledger.cable-anchor.org.

Financial reconciliation

Disbursements are reconciled against bank statements and the cause-level operating treasury at the close of each quarter.

An independent external audit is run twice per year by Casefield & Hurd, LLP. The 2024 audit closes 14 Feb 2025.

Carbon accounting

The Foundry does not issue carbon credits. The carbon outcomes of any project are recorded on the ledger but not sold or assigned to a third party.

All carbon estimates use the IPCC tier-3 protocol; field measurement supersedes modelled values.

Community accountability

Each project carries a community-of-record register. No disbursement is released without a community signatory present on the audit closure form.

Register entries are reviewed every cycle and rotated through the consortium board.
