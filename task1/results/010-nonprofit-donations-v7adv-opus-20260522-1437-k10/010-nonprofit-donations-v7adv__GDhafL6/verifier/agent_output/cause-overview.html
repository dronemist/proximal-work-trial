02About the cause
Steward team Field log ↗

The Foundry has worked with the Cardigan Bay saltmarsh consortium since June 2023. The programme links a long-running monitoring partnership with three coastal universities, a community-led tide-gate management agreement at Ynys-las, and a sediment-budget audit programme funded jointly with the Welsh Government.

Restoration capital is deployed in four-month cycles. Each cycle is preceded by a baseline assay, an audited expenditure plan, and a public field-note dispatch. Outcomes are scored on accretion rate, native-grass cover, invertebrate counts, and the secondary metric of community-trust survey response.

Every figure published on this overview is reconciled against the live ledger held in the Foundry treasury and is verifiable against the corresponding audit run identifier.

03Allocation of donor resources
By steward team By project Source data ↗
◢

Land first, narrative second

Title acquisition and conservation easements absorb the majority of cause capital. We do this first because every other intervention is a long-term commitment to a piece of land we now own.

◤

Permanence by covenant

Every acquired hectare carries a perpetual restoration covenant filed with the local registry. This is independently verified by the British Land & Water Trust before disbursement.

◇

Evidence against narrative

The Foundry's standing instruction to every steward is to publish negative findings within fourteen days. We have shut down four projects in three years on this basis, with full audit traceability.

◈

No greenwashing instruments

The Foundry will not accept funding tied to a brand offset, a verified-carbon credit, or any other mechanism whose primary economic function is to enable a continuing emission elsewhere.

◐

Local hands, local plans

Wherever a steward team operates, at least 60% of the programme budget is paid into local labour, local goods, and local advisory contracts — a covenant we re-verify each cycle.

◑

Public ledger & 4-month rhythm

Every penny in is reconciled, every penny out is bookable to a line item — and a steward dispatches a public field note before the next disbursement cycle begins. No exceptions.
