[01] position narrative · q2 2026

We finance the slow restoration of British and Atlantic-fringe ecosystems by translating long-cycle donor capital into hectare-grade stewardship work. The foundry holds twenty-seven active projects across four programme areas — saltmarsh, lowland bog, mixed broadleaf, and adjacent pastoral economies. Every hectare under our stewardship is enrolled in a two-decade monitoring window. Every monitored data point sits in this ledger.

FY24 closed with £18.4m deployed and an 8.7% overhead — beneath our published 12% ceiling for the seventh consecutive year. The figures below summarise the FY24 audit; the long form is available as a 142-page report, and every line item is open in the ledger.

[03] classifications of donor activity FY24 · audited
01 · land first42%
Land first, narrative second.

We do not own the land we restore. We finance its long-term lease, the species work, and the local labour that sustains it. £7.7m active.

02 · permanence26%
Permanence by covenant.

A site enters the foundry's stewardship only with a covenant on the land that outlives the project officer. £4.8m active.

03 · audit18%
Evidence against bullshit.

Every project lives or dies on its measurement plan. We publish the cores, the surveys, and the assumptions. £3.3m active.

04 · grow slowly14%
No greenwashing instruments.

We don't issue offsets. We don't trade carbon. The capital comes from donors, the work is for the place. £2.6m active.

05 · local hands11%
Local hands, local payroll.

76% of fieldwork hours go to residents within a 40-mile radius of the site. We list our subcontractors openly. £2.0m active.

06 · public9%
Public ledger or it didn't happen.

Every transaction over £25 is recorded with date, vendor, project, and signoff. CSV exports update each Sunday. £1.6m active.
