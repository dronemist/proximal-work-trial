[07] methodology how we measure what we measure
01
Field measurement.

Every site runs a quarterly transect protocol — cores, surveys, water-table loggers. Raw data is published with every quarterly entry. No third-party aggregator sits between the measurement and the page.

02
Financial reconciliation.

Every transaction over €25 is recorded in the ledger with vendor, date, project, and the staff signoff. Auditor reviews monthly. CSV available.

03
Carbon accounting.

We do not sell offsets. We do not trade credits. Carbon stocks are recorded as a benefit of the work, not as the currency. The ledger reports stocks against the IPCC 2019 refinement.

04
Community signoff.

Every project enrolls the surrounding community as named contributors. Crew lists are public. Local fieldwork hours are surfaced quarterly.
