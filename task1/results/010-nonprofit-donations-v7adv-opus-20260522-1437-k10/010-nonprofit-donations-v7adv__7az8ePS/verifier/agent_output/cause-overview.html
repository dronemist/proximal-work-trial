02 · RESTORATION APPROACH METHODS · V4.2

The foundry funds the slow work of putting landscapes back in function. Not gardens, not greenwashing — buffers, easements, channel recuts, planted-back saltmarsh. Every project is logged in a public ledger, every quarter we publish what worked and what didn't.

We pick our sites the same way we pick our crews — slowly, with reference checks, and never on the strength of a pitch deck. The result is a portfolio that compounds: 17% of FY24 capital is going into sites we first surveyed eight or more years ago, and we expect that share to grow.
