06 · FIELD STORIES CURATED · APRIL 2024
PLOT C-12 · WALES

Holding the line on the Dwyfor on Cardigan Bay.

Seven-year saltmarsh recovery has begun to outpace the 2017 sediment model. Crews report a 7.4 mm/yr accretion baseline.

FILE · GALWAY · IE

Six fortunes-anatery returns 1,072 ha to tidal exchange.

Bardún Mórr oyster tables and saltmarsh recuts have re-flooded a long-dry inland delta — survey work continues.

FĂGĂRAȘ · RO

Peat hydrology restored across the Tilbow headwaters.

1,402 ha of upper-basin Carpathian peat is back in service. Felling stopped two seasons ago; water table is up 18 cm.

08 · METHODOLOGY PUBLISHED · V4.2 · 14 APR
FIELD MEASUREMENT

Eight-quarter repeats

Each site is surveyed twice a quarter for the first three years, then biannually. Plots, transects and photo stations are fixed and published with the methods note.

FINANCIAL DISCIPLINE

Public ledger

All disbursements over £500 are posted within seven days. The ledger is reconciled monthly and signed by treasury, the director, and a board observer.

DATA & CODE

Methods directory

Survey workbooks, analysis scripts, and audit checklists live in a public Git repo. Forks are encouraged; we read pull requests.

DISCLOSURE

Quarterly digest

We publish a digest of failures, paused projects, and the four cases this year where we wrote a check we should not have. No omissions.
