PLOT C-12 · TIDAL GAIN
FIELD REPORT · 12 MAY 2024 · BY R. ASHTON

Three years on, the Cardigan Bay saltmarsh is holding sediment faster than projected.

Our three-year survey of Plot C-12 turned up sediment accretion at 7.4 mm/yr — meaningfully above the 4.1 mm/yr modelled in the 2021 baseline. Crews credit the recut tidal channels and the unusually warm winter of '23.

DISPATCH · 10 MAY 2024 · 6 MIN · BY J. ORTEGA

Beaver dam analogues at Glen Lewen: first measurable channel widening.

BDA-7 through BDA-13 have held since the autumn storms. The wetted footprint upstream is now 38% wider than the 2022 baseline, with riparian recruitment beginning at the south bank.

QUARTERLY · 06 MAY 2024 · 11 MIN · BY S. NAKAMURA

From the Carpathian front: thirty hectares of old-growth haunt was under perpetual protection.

Coordinated with the Cluj forestry directorate and three local cooperatives. The easement closes on June 1 and brings cumulative protected area in the Făgăraș range to 612 ha since 2019.

· EASEMENT · EUROPE · OLD GROWTH
METHODS · 28 APR 2024 · 14 MIN · BY R. ASHTON

Year-three seagrass audit, Loch Awe planting: 78.4% across 312,000 stems.

Survival outpaced our 60% planning floor. Density mapping shows where the substrate refused to take — gravel patches in the SW corner — and confirms the rest of the bay is on trajectory.
