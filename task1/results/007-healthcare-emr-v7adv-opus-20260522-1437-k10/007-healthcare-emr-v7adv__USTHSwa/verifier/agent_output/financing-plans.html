Common questions

Will this affect my credit?
No. In-house plans aren't reported to credit bureaus and there's no hard inquiry to start.
Can I pay off early?
Any time, no early-pay fees. You can also send extra against principal at any installment.
What if a month is tight?
Skip-one-month is included on your plan. Just let us know 3 days before your due date.
Do you offer external financing?
Yes — through CareCredit and Affirm. Their interest applies after the promo period.
Need to talk it through?
Aurelia at the front desk handles every plan very personally. Most questions take less than five minutes.
