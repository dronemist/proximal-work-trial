Common questions

Will this affect my credit?
No. Our in-house plans aren't reported to credit bureaus and there's no hard inquiry to enroll.
Can I pay off early?
Anytime. There are no prepayment penalties and your balance updates as soon as a payment posts.
What if a month is tight?
Each plan includes one skip-month — just tap "Adjust plan" or call the front desk.
Do you offer external financing?
Yes, for balances over $3,000. We partner with CareKey and Affirm; both offer 0% promo periods.
Need to talk it through?
Renee at the front desk handles every plan personally. Most questions take less than five minutes.
