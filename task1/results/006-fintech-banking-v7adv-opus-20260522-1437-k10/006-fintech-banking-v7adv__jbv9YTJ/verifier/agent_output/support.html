How do I freeze or replace a card I cannot find?−
Open the Cards page, select the affected card, and choose Freeze. A frozen card stops all new authorizations immediately, including contactless and recurring charges, but it does not affect refunds or pending settlements. If you are confident the card is lost, choose Replace from the same menu. A new card will be sent by tracked post within three business days.
