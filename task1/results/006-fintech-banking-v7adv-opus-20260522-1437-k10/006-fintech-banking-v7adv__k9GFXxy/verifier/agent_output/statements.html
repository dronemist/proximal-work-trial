A note on reconciliation

If a figure on a statement differs from your own records, please give the closing period a few business days to settle before raising it with us. Pending authorisations, foreign exchange holds, and merchant adjustments are reflected in the final document but may move during the soft-close window. When you do reach to dispute a line, the reference at the top right of any statement is the fastest way for our team to find it.
