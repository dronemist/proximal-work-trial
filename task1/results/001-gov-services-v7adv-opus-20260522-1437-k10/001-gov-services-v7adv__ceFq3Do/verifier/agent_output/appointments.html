What to bring

Skip a return trip. Most renewals only need three things; bring the fourth if you have it.

Required documents

  • Current driver's license (even if expired)
  • Proof of residency dated within 90 days
  • Cash OR Visa, bank statement, link, debit
  • Payment for the $34 renewal fee — card or check, no cash above $20

Helpful, not required

  • Eyeglasses or contacts if you wear them (we test vision on site)
  • Updated address or emergency contact
  • Organ donor preference (yes, no, or "ask me a little later")
  • A book. Honestly. Even on time, we run a little behind.
