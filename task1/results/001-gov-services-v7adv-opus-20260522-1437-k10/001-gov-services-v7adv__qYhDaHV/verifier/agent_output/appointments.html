What to bring

Skip a return trip. Have these ready before you head out.

Required documents
  • Current driver's license (even if expired)
  • Proof of residency dated within 90 days
  • Social Security card, last 4 digits OK
  • Payment for the $30 renewal fee — card or check, no cash above $20
Helpful, not required
  • Eyeglasses or contacts if you wear them (we test vision on site)
  • Updated address or emergency contact
  • Organ donor preference (yes, no, or "ask me later")
  • A book. Honestly. Even on time, we run a little behind.
