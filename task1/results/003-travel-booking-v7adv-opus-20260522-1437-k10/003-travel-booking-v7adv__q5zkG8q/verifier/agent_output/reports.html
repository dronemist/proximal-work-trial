CHAPTER I

A summer held its breath, and the rooms stayed full.

Mid-August's lull was easy to spot in the lobby — three quiet days, hard to track to anything you could pin. Average occupancy lifted to 78.4% over the quarter, fueled by August's late surge and a clear, repeated demand for the Faroe properties.

CHAPTER II

Rates lifted without strain, the market simply followed.

The three nine-increases were absorbed in February — Casa Encinal, then Kerkebosen, then Kura Mioka. Average nightly rate climbed to €412, a 4% increase from the same time last year. Bookings were unchanged from this same time last year.

CHAPTER VI

What the figures are not yet saying.

A reporting period is a narrow window. The studio's larger arc — five years of considered growth, a quietness around our editorial cadence, the loyalty of a few — does not fit cleanly in a table. There's still a great deal of meaning the studio still must read and tend.

The next statement, December's, will close the year and the chapter we begin.
