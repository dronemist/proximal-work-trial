Reading I

A summer held its breath, and the rooms stayed full.

Mid-July through mid-September held over 91% occupancy across the portfolio. The chief contributors were the Mediterranean stays in Liguria and Lipari — both stretching their seasons. The dip after Labour Day mirrors a gentle pricing reset.

Reading II

Rates lifted without strain, the market simply followed.

The first ADR increase appeared in February. A lighter, more honest, and more confident, with the help of repeat guests. ADR climbed 9.4% year over year, with the steepest gain in late August — a quiet pace, not a leap.

Reading VI

What the figures are not saying.

A reporting period is a matter of scale. The studio's larger view — five quarters in — sees an even quieter ascent than the headline. ADR has lifted by 9% across the year, but our hidden cost — energy in the Mediterranean, staff in the off-season — has lifted by 11. Net to host has thinned, by a margin small enough to ignore now, but not for the year after the year after.

Two new houses, returned this year. Continued careful planning, and the gentle.
