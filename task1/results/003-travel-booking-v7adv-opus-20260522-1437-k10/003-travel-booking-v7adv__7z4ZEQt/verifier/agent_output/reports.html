READING THE CURVE

A summer held its breath, and the rooms stayed full.

Mid-August broke the curve high, with three closures absorbed almost as soon as they were filled. The pattern carried into the second week of September — only one lull, at Maison Verre, when the kitchen was being restored. Occupancy then settled along its old shape.

READING THE BARS

Rates lifted without strain, the market simply followed.

We raised across the board in March, and the curve stayed broad. The strain that often follows a rate change — a longer lead time, more cancellations — did not appear this year, and so we kept the ceiling where it was.

READING THE LADDER

The portfolio ranks itself, top to bottom, by quiet preference.

Maison Verre leads, as it has every quarter since opening. Behind it, the steadier middle: the Cabin in Cap Ferret, the Cormorant, the new Casa Lumi. Atelier Nord — the most recently added — completes the ladder, though it brings up the bottom by design, not by accident.

Where the bookings come from, and what that says.

COMMITMENT

What the figures are not yet saying.

A repeating point is a routine practice. The studio's longer arc — the past quarter year out, the breaking of the slow rooms in winter, the new properties this spring — sits in a steadier curve, one not visible in a quarterly summary. We will return to it in the new year.

This was, however, a December's note that draws the year forward into the longer story.
