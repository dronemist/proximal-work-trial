FIELD NOTE NO. 12

The Black Barn returns to the spring calendar.

After a quiet winter — new flooring, a re-pointed chimney, and a re-thatched runway leading from the dunes — the Black Barn at Korenborg reopens its doors on the first of April. We've kept the booking window narrow this year, the better to let the place find its rhythm again.

FIELD NOTE NO. 09

Casa Encinal, between fog and fire.

Of all the houses in the portfolio, Casa Encinal is the one we think often photograph in fact in March — the wisteria has begun to throw itself across the walls, and the wood-burning oven is, of necessity, lit late into the afternoon.

The smoke wanders. The cats wander. The cypresses keep things tidy.
