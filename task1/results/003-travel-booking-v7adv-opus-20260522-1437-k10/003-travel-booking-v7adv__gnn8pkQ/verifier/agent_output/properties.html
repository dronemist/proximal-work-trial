Field Notes No. 7

Casa Encinal, between fog and fire.

Of all the houses in the portfolio, Casa Encinal is the one we most often photograph at four in the morning. The sea, the cliff, the kitchen lit up like an ember in the cold — a quiet drama, set nightly, attended by lichens. The interior is restored and rented but unhurried; we leave the lemons and the bread, the matches in the Spanish tin.

Architecture · Sebastián Quiroga, 1932
