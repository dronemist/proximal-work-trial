Folio №18 · Featured

The Black Barn returns to the spring calendar.

After a quiet winter — new flashing, a re-pointed chimney, and a wine fridge — the Black Barn opens its doors again on the first of March. The new larder is stocked with anchovies, semolina, and a great deal of the slow vinegar Henrik keeps making in jars.

  • Reopening — Saturday, 1 March
  • Three-night minimum, spring
  • Steward — Henrik Kappel
Folio №19 · Letting Begins

Casa Encinal, between fog and fire.

Of all the houses in the portfolio, Casa Encinal is the one we most often photograph at four in the morning. The new shutters wear their second coat, the linen has finished its weeks-long process in the rosemary salts, and the kitchen — long planned — is finally open for cooking.

  • Three-night minimum, summer
