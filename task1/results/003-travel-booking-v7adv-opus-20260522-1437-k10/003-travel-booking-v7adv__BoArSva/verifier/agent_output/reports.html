The quarter, read in figures and in feeling.

A measured account of how the portfolio performed between July and September — occupancy held by season, average rates lifted by deliberation, and the quiet ascent of the studio's nine stays.

Reading the line

A summer held its breath, and the rooms stayed full.

Mid-July through mid-September held above 86% occupancy — the studio's tightest sustained interval. The dotted line tracks the same nine weeks in 2023; this year ran a quiet but consistent five points above. Maison Verre at Antibes and Pinta Verova at Carrer Avgut drove the gain.

Average Rate

Rates lifted without strain, the market simply followed.

The bone-deep increase came from a fourteen-percent lift in the shoulder season — September shoulders, mostly. Across the year we sit at 6% above last, with no measurable softening in conversion rate.

Reading the bars

The portfolio ranks itself, top to bottom, by quiet preference.

Maison Verre carries the studio for a third quarter running. Six houses cleared the €300 RevPAR line — twice the count last autumn — while Astoria Loft and the Black Barn both sit on a curve of recovery. Black Barn was offline twelve nights for the chimney rebuild, which explains the August.

Channel Mix

Where the bookings come from, and what that says.

Direct reservations through the studio site again did the heaviest carrying — 49% of nights, 55% of revenue. Airbnb arrivals stayed slightly shorter on average, with cleaner conversion. The private referral list, small and slow, brought twenty-two of the year's longest stays.

Closing Reading

What the figures are not saying.

A reporting period is a narrow window. The studio's longer arc — the slow turn of a portfolio across years — does not show in this table. So we close with a few notes for the rooms themselves: where the linens are, when the chimney is next due, who covered the desk in August. The hands behind the columns.

This year, especially, December's the one we ought to read with care. Holiday closings, two refit weeks at Casa Lottè, and the cold snap in the north all read here as softness; they are not. They are the next quarter's gift.
