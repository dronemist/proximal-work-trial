Danger zone

These actions are permanent. Pause and read carefully.
Export all workspace data
A signed download link will be available for 24 hours. Includes contacts, listings, notes, and documents.
Transfer workspace ownership
Hand principal-broker controls to another team member. Requires owner re-verification.
Close workspace
Cancels billing and deletes all data after a 30-day grace window. Cannot be undone after day 31.
