Analyst notes

Headline
The quarter closed firmer than the spring layer suggested. Median pricing moved up, days on market compressed across the mid band, and Lakeside Commons reset its own ceiling twice.
Watch
Active count remains 12.4% below the year-ago figure. We expect a modest October bump — eleven sellers we've spoken with are timing listings for the first two weeks of the month.
Risk
Westmoor softening: the only neighborhood with negative YoY price movement and a sale-to-list under 97%. Worth a targeted CMA outreach to owners over 60 months tenure.
Recommend
Re-price the three Q2 carry-overs (Birch Lane, Aspen Court, 14B & Surrey) before Oct 19, staging refresh on the two listings sitting above 40 days.
