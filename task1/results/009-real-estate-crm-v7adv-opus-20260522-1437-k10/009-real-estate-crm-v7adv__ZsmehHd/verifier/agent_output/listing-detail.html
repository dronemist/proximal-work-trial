About this property

Tucked at the quiet end of Linden Row, this 1996 craftsman has been thoughtfully kept by a single family since 2004. White oak floors run through the main level, with a renovated kitchen (2021) opening onto a screened porch that catches the afternoon light. The primary suite was reconfigured last year to add a walk-in closet and a reading nook. Mechanicals are current: roof replaced 2022, HVAC dual-zone 2019, on-demand water heater 2023. Sellers prefer a 45-day close and are flexible on rent-back through July 15.
