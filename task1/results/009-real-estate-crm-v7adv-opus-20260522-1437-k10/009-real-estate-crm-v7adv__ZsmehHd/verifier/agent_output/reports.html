Analyst notes

HEADLINE

The quarter closed firmer than the spring lean suggested. Median pricing moved up, days on market compressed across the mid band, and Lakeside Commons reset its own ceiling.

INVENTORY

Active count remains 11% below the year-ago figure. We expect a modest October bump — eleven sellers we've spoken with are listing listings for the first two weeks of the month.

PRICING

Westmont softening: the only neighborhood with negative YoY price movement and a sale-to-list rate under 97%. Worth a targeted CMA outreach to owners over 60 months tenure.

RECOMMENDATIONS

Re-price the three Q2 carry-overs (Birch Lane, Aspen Court, 14B & Surrey) before Oct 14, staging refresh on the two listings staging on the rim.
