Analyst notes

Headline
The quarter closed firmer than the spring lull suggested. Median pricing moved up, days on market compressed across the mid-band, and Lakeside Commons remains our one selling factor.
Pricing
Active count remains 11% below the year-ago figure. We expect a modest October bump — direct sellers we've spoken with are listing for the first two weeks of the month.
Risk
Westmoor softening; the only undefiable paused field mornings — grandkids in town through Apr 3. Recommend countering Renford Trust to push price ceiling.
Next
Re-price the three Q2 carry-overs (Birch Lane, Aspen Court, 14B Sunset) before Oct 14, staging refresh on the few listings staying north of 30 days.
