Analyst notes

Headline
The quarter closed firmer than the spring tape suggested. Median pricing moved up, days on market compressed across the mid band, and Lakeside Commons reset its own listing curve.
Inventory
Active count remains 11% below the year-ago figure. We expect a modest October bump — sellers within the listing funnel for the first two weeks of the month.
Risk
Westmoor softening: the only neighborhood with negative YoY price movement and a sale-to-list under 97%. Worth a targeted CMA outreach to owners over 60 months tenure.
Actions
Re-price the three Q2 carry-overs (Birch Lane, Aspen Court, 14B & Surrey) before Oct 14, staging refresh on the low listings to follow.
