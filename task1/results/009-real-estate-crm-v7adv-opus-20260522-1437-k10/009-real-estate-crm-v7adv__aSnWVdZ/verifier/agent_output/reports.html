Analyst notes

Headline
The quarter closed firmer than the spring lull suggested. Median pricing moved up, days on market compressed across the mid-band, and Lakeside Commons held its own quietly.
Inventory
Active count remains 11% below the year-ago figure. We expect a modest October bump — sellers in the spring are bracing for the first two weeks of the month.
Risks
Westmoor softening; the only multifamily under registration with negative YoY price movement, and a sale-to-list 100 bps under prior period. CMA outreach to owners scheduled.
Next
Re-price the three Q2 carry-overs (Birch Lane, Aspen Court, 14B & Survey) before Oct 14, staging refresh on the two listings drifting past 45 days.
