Analyst notes

Headline
The quarter closed firmer than the spring lows suggested. Median pricing moved up, days on market compressed across the mid band, and Lakeside Commons even saw cooling values.
Watch
Active count remains 12% below the year-ago figure. We expect a modest October bump — when sellers want to be settled before the holidays and inventory listings for the first two weeks of the month.
Risks
Westmoor softening: the only neighborhood with negative YoY price movement and a buyer pipeline that's narrower than mid-2023. Recommend a renewed outreach to the side under 5%. Worth a targeted CMA outreach to owners over 12 months in.
Next
Re-price the three Q2 carry-overs (Birch Lane, Asper Court, 14th & Surrey) before Oct 19, staging refresh on the two listings staying on the market past 40 days.
