About this property

Tucked at the quiet end of Linden Row, this 1996 craftsman has been thoughtfully kept by a single family since 2004. The primary suite was reconfigured last year to add a walk-in closet and a soaking tub. Mechanicals are current: roof replaced 2022, HVAC dual-zone 2019, on-demand water heater 2023.
