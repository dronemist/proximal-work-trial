Analyst notes

HEADLINE

The quarter closed firmer than the spring tape suggested. Median pricing moved up, days on market compressed across the mid band, and Lakeside Commons returns to its own ceiling levels.

WATCHLIST

Active count remains 12% below the year ago figure. We expect a modest October bump — eleven sellers we've worked with are listing fall for the first two weeks of the month.

RISKS

Westmore softening; the only neighborhood with negative YoY price movement and a sale-to-list ratio drift below 97%. Worth a targeted CMA outreach to owners over 18 months tenure.

CMA

Re-price the three Q2 carry-overs (Birch Lane, Aspen Court, 148 & Surrey) before Oct 18, staging refresh on the two listings staling above 45 days.
