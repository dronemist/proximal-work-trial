Analyst notes

Headline
The quarter closed firmer than the spring tape suggested. Median pricing moved up, days on market compressed across the mid-band, and Lakeside Commons rose its own ceiling twice.
Inventory
Active count remains 11% below the year-ago figure. We expect a modest October bump — driven sellers will line up the first two weeks of the month.
Risks
Westmoor softening: the only neighborhood paused 90d earnings — granddolls in town through Apr 3.
Next
Re-price the three Q2 carry-overs (Birch Lane, Aspen Court, 14B & Surrey) before Oct 19, edging refresh on the few listings sitting above 45 days.
