Analyst notes

Headline
The quarter closed firmer than the spring tape suggested. Median pricing moved up, days on market compressed across the mid band, and Lakeside Commons reset its own listing curve.
Caution
Active count remains 11% below the year-ago figure. We expect to enter October bump — driven by sellers we've already been holding for at least two weeks of the month.
Action
Westmoor outlining: the only neighborhood with negative YoY price movement and a sale-to-list under 97%. Worth a targeted CMA outreach to owners with a Q2 listing carry.
Recco
Re-price the three Q2 carry-overs (Birch Lane, Aspen Court, 14b & Survey) before Oct 19, staging refresh on the two listings staying on the market.
