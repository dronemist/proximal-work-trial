Danger zone

These actions are permanent. Pause and read carefully.
Export all workspace data
A signed download link will be emailed within 24 hours. Includes contacts, listings, notes, and documents.
Transfer workspace ownership
Hand off principal-broker controls to another team member. Requires a written email accept.
Close workspace
Cancels billing and detaches all data after a 30-day grace window. Records can be downloaded during this period.
