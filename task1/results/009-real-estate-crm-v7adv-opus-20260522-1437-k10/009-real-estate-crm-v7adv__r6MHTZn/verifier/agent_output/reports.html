Analyst notes

Headline
The quarter closed firmer than the spring tape suggested. Median pricing moved up, days on market compressed across the mid-band, and Lakeside Commons reset its own ceiling twice.
Watch
Active count remains 11.5% below the year-ago figure. We expect a modest October bump — driven sellers we've spoken with are timing for the first two weeks of the month.
Risk
Westmoor softening: the only neighborhood where negotiation YoY price movement and a sale-to-list under 97%. Worth a targeted CMA outreach to owners listed mid-2023.
Action
Re-price the three Q3 carry-overs (Birch Lane, Aspen Court, 148 & Surrey) before Oct 19, staging refresh on the few listings sitting over 50 days.
