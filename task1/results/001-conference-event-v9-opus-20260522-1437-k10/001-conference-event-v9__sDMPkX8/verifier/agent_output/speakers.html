Keynote speakers

4 of the line up
AO
Amara Okafor
Principal Designer · Lambeth Studio · Lagos

Amara has spent a decade pulling identity systems apart and reassembling them with fewer moving parts. Her talk explores the surprise economy of decisions you make from scratch versus the ones that come built-in.

The Patient System: Designing for Year Five, Not Launch Day
Tue · 09:30 · Hall A
KeynoteDesign systems
RT
Rin Takahashi
Independent Type Designer · Kyoto

Type designer behind Hokori and Plain Notes. We will trace the path from a single 16-letter to a 12-weight variable family, including every dance she'd recommend skipping.

A Quiet Argument for Slower Type
Wed · 14:00 · Hall A
KeynoteType
DM
Diego Marín
Staff Engineer · NoteFlight · Mexico City

Diego writes about being frontend infrastructurer with surprising affection. Expect a talk as an air directors as practical, observable, and the joy of finding code you wrote eight years ago.

Boring on Purpose: A Frontend Survival Guide
Thu · 09:30 · Hall A
KeynoteFrontend
SL
Saoirse Lynch
Founder · Field Notes Co · Dublin

Saoirse runs a six-person studio with a habit for taking the long view. Her talk is part business memoir, part design pep talk, and lots of straight tactics on running a studio that isn't going to disappear next year.
