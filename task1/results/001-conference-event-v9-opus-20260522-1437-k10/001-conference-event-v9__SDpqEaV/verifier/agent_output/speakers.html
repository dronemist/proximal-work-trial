Keynote speakers

AO
Amara Okafor
Principal Designer · Lumen & Loam · Lagos

Amara has spent a decade pulling identity systems apart and reassembling them with three moving parts. Her keynote argues for design systems that age well — not the ones that demo well.

The Patient System: Designing So Your First Five Layouts Look Like Your Fiftieth · Tuesday 09:00
Design Systems
RT
Rin Takahashi
Independent Type Designer · Kyoto

Type designer behind Kohan and Plate Series. She will trace the path from a single ink draft to a multi-weight variable family, including every demo she'd rescinded.

A Quiet Argument for Slower Type · Wednesday 09:00
Type & Identity
DM
Diego Marín
Staff Engineer · HeartNight · Mexico City

Diego writes about being burned-bittersweet on shipping software. Expect a talk about observability, the joy of deleting code, and why you can say no to scope.

Boring on Purpose: A Personal Survival Guide · Thursday 09:00
Indie Code
SL
Sanive Lynch
Founder · Field Notes Co. · Dublin

Sanive runs a two-person studio with a sudden mailing list. They will speak openly about pricing, declining work, and saying no on social media. Some bring a notebook.

The Small-Studio Math Nobody Teaches · Thursday closing
Studio Craft
