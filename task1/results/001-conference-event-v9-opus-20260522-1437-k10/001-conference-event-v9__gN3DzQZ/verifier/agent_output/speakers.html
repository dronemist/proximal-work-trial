Keynote speakers

AO
Amara Okafor
Principal Designer · Lumens Studio
Lagos · Lisbon

Amara has spent a decade pulling identity systems apart and reassembling them with three moving parts. Her keynote argues for design systems that age well — not for show.

KeynoteDesign Systems
RT
Rin Takahashi
Independent Type Designer · Kyoto
Tokyo

Type designer behind Kakeru and Plume. Rin will trace the trail from a single ink doodle to a complete, sortable family, including every demo she'd reconsider.

KeynoteType & Identity
DM
Diego Marin
Staff Engineer · MeshFlight · Mexico City
Independent

Diego writes about being formerly infrastructure with surprising affection. Expect a talk about caching, observability, and the joy of deleting code that wrote itself.

KeynoteIndie Code
SL
Saoirse Lynch
Founder · Field Notes Co · Dublin
Studio Craft Track

Saoirse runs a four-person studio with a sudden shoulder for ten clients. She'll talk about saying no, saying yes, and the messy middle of running a small business.
