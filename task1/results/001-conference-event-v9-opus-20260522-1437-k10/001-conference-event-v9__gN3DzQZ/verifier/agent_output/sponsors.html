Sponsor with intent

Sponsorship at Ember Vector Works is small by design.

No keynote-sales pitches, no sign-stickering. We cap each tier, write program copy together, and share attendance numbers the week after the event. If that sounds like your kind of partnership, the prospectus is a short read.

What partners get

  • A written sponsor brief, co-edited with our editorial team
  • Onsite booth in the Mezzanine, all three days
  • Attendee opt-in lead capture (no scraping)
  • Post-event recap with demographics and feedback session
  • Invitation to the sponsor dinner on opening night
