Keynote speakers

4 sessions confirmed
AO
Amara Okafor
Principal Designer · Lumenoir Studio · Lagos
Amara has spent a decade pulling identity systems apart and reassembling them with fewer moving parts. Her keynote argues that design systems that age well — not the ones that scale to be more.
KeynoteDesign Systems
The Patient Format: Designing for Year Five, Not Launch
RT
Rin Takahashi
Independent Type Designer · Kyoto
Type designer behind Yokai and Plume Series. We will trace the path from a single ink scribble to a 12-weight variable family, including every dance she'd recommend skipping.
KeynoteType
A Quiet Argument for Slower Type
DM
Diego Marin
Staff Engineer · IndieFlight · Mexico City
Diego writes about being framed Infrastructure with surprising aplomb. Expect a talk about caching, observability, and the joy of deleting code you wrote ten years ago.
KeynoteTooling
Boring on Purpose: A Personal Service Guide
SL
Santee Lynch
Founder · Field Notes Co · Dublin
Santee runs a ten-person studio with a sudden longer-than-you-want plan and an approach. She writes occasionally as gowned wires.
KeynoteStudio Practice
The Small Studio Hold Velocity Tracks
