Keynote speakers

4 of 4 invited
AO
Amara Okafor
Principal Designer · Lumitech Studio · Lagos
"Tokens that survive the second redesign"

Amara has spent a decade pulling identity systems apart and reassembling them with better seams. Her keynote opens day one with the case that done isn't real.

Keynote Day 1 Design Systems
RT
Rin Takahashi
Independent Type Designer · Kyoto
"A Quiet Argument for Slower Type"

Type designer behind Sofue and Plant Series. We will trace the path to a Latin- single life in a 12-weight variable family, including every detour she'd recommend skipping.

Keynote Day 2 Type
DM
Diego Marín
Staff Engineer · HelioFlight · Mexico City
"Doing as Purpose: A Functional Survival Guide"

Diego writes about being frontend infrastructure with surprising affection. Expect a talk about building, observability, and the joy of deleting code you wrote six months ago.

Keynote Day 3 Tooling
SL
Saoirse Lynch
Founder · Field Notes Co. · Dublin
"The Small Studio Holds Tools"

Saoirse runs a one-person studio with a stable client list and zero burnout playbook. She moves occasionally as ghostwriter, often as the editor and trainer of a generation.
