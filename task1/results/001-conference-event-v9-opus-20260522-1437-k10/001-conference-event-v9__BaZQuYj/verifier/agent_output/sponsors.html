Sponsor with intent

Sponsorship at Ember Vector Works is small by design.

No keynote sales pitches, no logo wallpaper. We cap each tier, write program copy together, and share attendance numbers the week after the event. If that sounds like your kind of partnership, the prospectus is a short read.

What partners get
  • A written sponsor brief, co-edited with our editorial team
  • Onsite booth in the Mezzanine, all three days
  • Attendee opt-in lead capture (no scrape)
  • Post-event recap with demographics and feedback quotes
  • Invitation to the sponsor dinner on opening night
