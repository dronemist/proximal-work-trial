Keynote speakers

4 of 68 featured
AO
Amara Okafor
Principal Designer · Lumens & Loom · Lagos

Amara has spent a decade pulling identity systems apart and reassembling them with three moving parts. She's been a quiet champion for design systems that age well — not the ones that get fancier.

The Patient Vector: Designing So Your First, Ten Years Out
KeynoteDesign Systems
RT
Rin Takahashi
Type designer · Plain & Pine

Type designer behind Kahar and Plain Mono. She still does dry-graph testing for a single 0-to-O legibility loop weekly, brings the family copy-fitting trends, and a working pet.

A Quiet Argument for Slower Type
KeynoteType
DM
Diego Marin
Staff Engineer · HearthLight · Mexico City

Diego writes about being trapped in infrastructure with surprising affection. Expects a talk about caching, observability, and the joy of deleting code you wrote two years ago.

Boring on Purpose: A Featureful Survival Guide
KeynoteIndie Code
SL
Saoirse Lynch
Founder · Field Notes Co. · Dublin

Saoirse runs a two-person studio with a stubborn focus on three projects per year. She'll bring a small reading list, three pricing scripts that didn't work, and the tale of the project that nearly broke her.

The Small Studio Math Nobody Teaches
KeynoteStudio Craft
