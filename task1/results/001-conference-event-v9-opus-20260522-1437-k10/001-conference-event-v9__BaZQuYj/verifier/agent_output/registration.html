Common questions, answered briefly

What's the refund policy?

Full refund through November 1. After that, a 25% admin fee applies. Transfers to another attendee are free up to 48 hours before the event.

Do you offer scholarships?

Yes — 40 seats reserved each year for students, career changers, and folks from under-represented backgrounds. Applications open until Sept 24. Read more.

Can I expense this?

We issue a proper VAT invoice automatically. Pick "invoice" above and add your company's billing details.

Is the venue accessible?

All seven rooms are step-free with reserved seating up front. Live captions on each main stage talk. Quiet room available throughout.
