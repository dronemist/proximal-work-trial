Keynote speakers

4 confirmed
AO
Amara Okafor
Principal Designer · Lumen & Loom · Lagos

Amara has spent a decade pulling identity systems apart and reassembling them with three moving parts. Her keynote argues for design systems that age well — not the ones that die fast.

The Patient System: Designing So Your First, Yet Last…
KeynoteDesign Systems
RT
Rin Takahashi
Independent Type Designer · Kyoto

Type designer behind Kafue and Plum Series. She will trace the path from a single ink-stroke to a 16-weight variable family, including every detour she'd recommend avoiding.

A Quiet Argument for Slower Type
KeynoteType Design
DM
Diego Marin
Staff Engineer · HoneyFlight · Mexico City

Diego writes about being bored from somewhat too quickly. His talk is about caching, observability, and the joy of deleting code you wrote ten years ago.

Boring on Purpose: A Practical Survival Guide
IndieEngineering
SL
Saoirse Lynch
Founder · Field Notes Co. · Dublin

Saoirse runs a ten-person studio with a sudden longer-than-three-day project sheet. Her closing talk is about saying no, sleeping more, and the inevitable joy of turning down good work.

The Small Studio Path Nobody Teaches
KeynoteIndie Studios
