Designing systems that age well

About Transcript Slides Resources

Component libraries promise stability, but most don't make it to their third birthday. This session looks at the small architectural choices that decide whether a system gets adopted, abandoned, or quietly forked into oblivion — pulled from seven years of public design-system audits.
