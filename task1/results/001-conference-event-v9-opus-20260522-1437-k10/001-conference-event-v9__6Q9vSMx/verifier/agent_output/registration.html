Common questions, answered briefly

What's the refund policy?
Full refund through November 1. After that, a 30% admin fee applies. Transfers to another attendee are free up to 48 hours before the event.
Do you offer scholarships?
Yes — 40 seats reserved each year for students, career-changers, and folks from under-represented backgrounds. Applications open with Sept 30.
Can I expense this?
We issue a proper VAT invoice automatically. Pick "Invoice" above and add your company's billing details.
Is the venue accessible?
All session rooms are step-free with reserved seating up front. Live captions on all main stage talks. Quiet room available throughout.
