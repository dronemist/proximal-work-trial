SPONSOR WITH INTENT

Sponsorship at Ember Vector Works is small by design.

No keynote sales pitches, no logo wallpaper. We cap each tier, write program copy together, and share attendance reports the week after the event. If that sounds like your kind of partnership, the prospectus is a short read.

Email the partnerships team

What partners get

  • A written sponsor brief, co-edited with our editorial team
  • Onsite booth in the Mezzanine, all three days
  • Attendee opt-in lead capture (no scraping)
  • Post-event recap with demographics and feedback session
  • Invitation to the sponsors dinner on opening night
