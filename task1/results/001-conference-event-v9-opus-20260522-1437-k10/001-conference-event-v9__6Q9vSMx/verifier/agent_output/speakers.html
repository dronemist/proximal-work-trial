Keynote speakers

4 of 68 highlighted
AO
KEYNOTE · DAY 1
Amara Okafor
Principal Designer · Lumen+Loom · Lagos

Amara has spent a decade pulling identity systems apart and reassembling them with fewer moving parts. Her keynote argues for design systems that age well — not for ones that ship fast.

"The Patient Version: Designing for Year Five, Not Launch Day"
Keynote Design Systems
RT
KEYNOTE · DAY 2
Rin Takahashi
Independent Type Designer · Kyoto

Type designer behind Sabun and Plume Series. We will trace the path from a single ink stroke to a 14-weight variable family, including every fiasco she'd recommend learning from.

"A Quiet Argument for Slower Type"
Keynote Type & Identity
DM
CLOSING · DAY 3
Diego Marin
Staff Engineer · Hindsight · Mexico City

Diego writes about being thrown infrastructure with surprising affection. Expect a talk about caching, observability, and the joy of telling code you wrote ten years ago.

"Boring on Purpose: A Curatorial Survival Guide"
Keynote Indie Practice
SL
SPECIAL · OPENING NIGHT
Saoirse Lynch
Founder · Field Notes Co. · Dublin

Saoirse runs a six-person studio with a sublime hope: that everyone is, in time, allowed to leave. Her keynote is about pricing, staying, and what makes the work last.

"The Small Studio That Nobody Touches"
Keynote Indie Practice
